Methodology

Dealer takedowndealer accepted ÷ (competitive + non-competitive accepted)Primary dealers must bid in every auction, so a high share means the open market held back.
Deviation(takedown − mean of prior 12) ÷ stdev of prior 12Each tenor is scored against its own history: 32% is normal for a bill and alarming for a 10-year.
Fed rollovers excludedSOMA amounts are left out of both sides of the ratio and published separately, so the Fed's maturity calendar can't pass for investor appetite.
ScopeBills, fixed-rate notes and bonds, reopenings included. Excluded: TIPS, floating-rate notes and cash management bills. Full reasoning in the README →

How to read this

Dealer takedown is the share of public demand that primary dealers absorbed: dealer ÷ (competitive + non-competitive). Each auction is turned into a deviation against the 12 prior auctions of the same tenor, which makes bills and bonds comparable.

Fed (SOMA) rollovers are excluded from both sides of the ratio and published separately. TIPS, FRNs and cash management bills are out of scope. Bid dispersion is high minus median yield, not a tail. Full methodology →
